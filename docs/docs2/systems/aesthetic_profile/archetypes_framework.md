# Aesthetic Profile: Archetypes Framework

## Summary
Nine archetypes capture the major families of architectural taste: Classicist, Romantic, Stylist, Modernist, Industrialist, Visionary, Pop Culturalist, Vernacularist, Austerist. Two subtypes: Infrastructuralist (Industrialist) and Naturalist (Vernacularist).

## Highlights
- Classicist: symmetry, proportion, noble materials
- Romantic: narrative, eclecticism, atmosphere
- Stylist: surface, pattern, glamour
- Modernist: clarity, function, truth to materials
- Industrialist: exposed structure, utility, patina
- Visionary: formal experimentation, tech-forward
- Pop Culturalist: spectacle, legibility, commercial semiotics
- Vernacularist: locality, climate intelligence, craft
- Austerist: efficiency, standards, cost logic

## Relationships
Natural affinities: Classicist↔Stylist, Modernist↔Industrialist, Romantic↔Vernacularist, Industrialist↔Austerist
Oppositions: Modernist↔Romantic, Austerist↔Stylist, Classicist↔Industrialist, Vernacularist↔Pop Culturalist
