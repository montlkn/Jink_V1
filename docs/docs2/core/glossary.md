# Glossary

## Core Terms
**Orb** — live 3D representation of the user’s aesthetic profile.
**Scan** — act of identifying a building via camera + CLIP.
**Dérive / Jink** — generated walk personalized to taste.
**Stamp** — collectible mark for unique building discovery.
**Passport** — collection of user memories, lists, and achievements.
**XP** — experience points; measure of progress and exploration.
**Archetype** — one of nine stylistic archetypes used in profiling.
**Edge Function** — Supabase serverless function triggered by events.
**Profile Drift** — small vector adjustment from new data.

> *Words are the scaffolding of meaning.*
