# Product: Pitch

## Summary
Imagine walking through a city and instantly knowing the story behind every facade. JINK lets you point your camera at a building and see its hidden layers of history, design, and cultural DNA.

## Value Proposition
- Visual search for architecture
- Personalized dérives
- A living aesthetic profile

## Audience
Design enthusiasts, travelers, locals rediscovering their city.

## Vision
Reignite curiosity about the built world using AI as an interpretive lens.

> *The city is the museum; your eye is the curator.*
